This policy explains cookies and similar technologies that may be used when you visit Axiam Technologies' website or services. It supplements our Privacy Policy.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, and identifiers that store or access information on a device. Session cookies generally expire when the session ends; persistent cookies remain for a defined period or until deleted. First-party cookies are set by the site you visit, while third-party cookies are set by another provider.

2. Categories of technologies

The following are standard categories, not a claim that every category is used on this website:

  • Strictly necessary: support functions such as secure sessions, authentication, fraud prevention, and remembering privacy choices. Where a legal exemption applies, these do not require consent.
  • Preferences: remember optional settings such as language or display choices. Consent may be required depending on the purpose and applicable law.
  • Analytics: help measure how visitors use a website and identify performance issues. Where consent is required, these must not be activated before consent.
  • Advertising: support advertising measurement or personalization. Where consent is required, these must not be activated before consent.

3. Consent requirements

Where required by applicable law, optional cookies and similar technologies should be used only after you give informed consent. Rejecting optional tracking must be available, and withdrawing consent should be as easy as giving it. Continued browsing is not, by itself, valid consent. Strictly necessary technologies may remain enabled only where an applicable exemption covers their use.

This policy does not itself provide a consent control or activate, disable, or remove tracking. Any consent settings presented by the website should accurately reflect the technologies deployed.

4. Managing cookies

You can use your browser's privacy settings to inspect, block, or delete cookies and manage stored website data. Browser controls vary, so consult your browser's help documentation. Blocking necessary cookies may prevent sign-in or other requested functions from working. Deleting cookies may also remove saved preferences. Browser settings are not a substitute for obtaining consent where the law requires it, and they may not control every similar technology.

5. Third-party services and external links

Third-party services or embedded content may store or access information on your device, depending on the features used and applicable consent requirements. External sites, including the Axiam dashboard, may have their own cookie practices. Consult the relevant provider's policies. An external link does not, by itself, mean that the external site's cookies are set on this website.

6. Cookie inventory and duration

Specific cookie names, providers, and expiry periods are not listed in this policy. Session and persistent storage durations depend on the relevant technology and its configuration.

7. Personal data and transfers

Where cookies or similar technologies process personal data, applicable privacy rules govern the processing, sharing, retention, and any international transfers. See our Privacy Policy and GDPR Policy for further information about data protection and your rights.

8. Changes and contact

This policy should be updated when the website's technologies or applicable requirements change. For questions about cookies or privacy, contact Axiam Technologies at support@axiam.io.
