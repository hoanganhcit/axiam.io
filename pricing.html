Frequently Asked Questions

How do you count MAUs?

A unique user who completes at least one successful authentication or encryption operation in a monthly billing period counts as one MAU.

Is the Free tier really free?

Yes. You can use up to 5,000 MAUs across services without charge to prototype and launch.

What about compliance?

Enterprise plans can include support for SOC 2, GDPR, and HIPAA depending on scope and data flows.

Do you offer annual discounts?

Yes — speak with sales for usage-based annual commitments and savings.
