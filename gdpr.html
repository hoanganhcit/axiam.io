This policy explains how Axiam Technologies approaches the protection of personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and, where applicable, the UK GDPR. It supplements our Privacy Policy. These laws apply only where their territorial and other requirements are met; this document is not a certification of compliance.

1. Scope and responsibilities

This policy covers personal data associated with our website, inquiries, job applications, accounts, and Facial Sign-In, FaceLock Encryption, and Bastion with Facial Sign-In services. Axiam generally acts as a controller when deciding why and how data is processed for its own business purposes. When processing data on an organization's documented instructions, Axiam may act as a processor and the organization remains the controller. The applicable service agreement and data processing agreement should define those responsibilities.

2. Personal data

Depending on your interaction with Axiam, data may include your name, email address, organization, account details, communications, application documents, device information, IP address, usage logs, and facial images or biometric templates used for authentication.

Biometric data used to uniquely identify an individual is special-category personal data under GDPR and requires additional protection.

3. Purposes and lawful bases

A lawful basis must be identified for each processing purpose. Depending on the circumstances, these may include:

  • Contract: delivering requested services or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • Legitimate interests: proportionate security, fraud prevention, and business administration, where those interests do not override your rights and freedoms.
  • Legal obligation: meeting applicable legal requirements.
  • Consent: processing that requires your freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous agreement, including applicable optional tracking or marketing.

Processing special-category biometric data also requires an Article 9 condition, such as explicit consent where valid and appropriate, in addition to an Article 6 lawful basis. Consent must not be assumed merely because someone uses the service. Where consent is relied upon, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

4. Sharing and service providers

Personal data may be disclosed to authorized service providers supporting the services, to your organization as required to provide its service, or where disclosure is required by law. Processing arrangements should limit access to the purpose of the engagement and impose appropriate confidentiality and data protection obligations. See our Privacy Policy for additional information about sharing.

5. International transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA or UK, a valid transfer mechanism is required, such as an applicable adequacy decision, EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or appropriate UK transfer instruments. Any necessary transfer assessment and supplementary measures should be completed. You may contact us for information about the safeguards applicable to your data.

6. Retention and security

Personal data should be kept only as long as necessary for the stated purpose, applicable contractual requirements, and legal obligations, then securely deleted or anonymized. Appropriate technical and organizational measures should reflect the sensitivity and risks of processing, particularly for biometric data. Our Privacy Policy describes our security and deletion practices. Specific retention periods should be confirmed for each service and data category.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable conditions and exceptions, you may request:

  • Access to your personal data and information about its processing.
  • Correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure of your data.
  • Restriction of processing.
  • Portability of eligible data.
  • An objection to processing based on legitimate interests, and an objection to direct marketing at any time.
  • Withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent.

GDPR also provides protections relating to solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects, where applicable. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Requests should normally receive a response within one month; where permitted, this may be extended by up to two further months with an explanation. Where your organization is the controller, requests may need to be directed to it, with Axiam assisting as appropriate.

8. Complaints and incidents

You have the right to complain to the relevant supervisory authority, including in the EEA country where you habitually reside, work, or where an alleged infringement occurred, or to the UK Information Commissioner's Office where applicable. You do not need to contact us first.

Where a personal data breach occurs, applicable GDPR obligations include notifying the competent authority within 72 hours of becoming aware when required, and informing affected individuals without undue delay when the breach is likely to result in a high risk.

9. Contact and updates

For privacy questions or rights requests, contact Axiam Technologies at support@axiam.io. Any legally required data protection officer or EU/UK representative details should be provided once designated. This policy may be updated to reflect changes to our processing or applicable law.
