SECURITY GUARANTEES

Engineered so only you can get in.

Every feature exists for one purpose: to make it technically impossible for anyone other than the real account owner to authenticate.

Only YOU Can Access Your Account

Axiam's biometric identity is cryptographically bound to your unique facial structure. No one else — not even with your photo or a deepfake — can impersonate you.

Account Sharing? Impossible.

Since authentication requires the real, live face of the registered user, accounts simply cannot be shared. Your license is yours. Your data stays yours.

Hackers Have Nothing to Steal

There are no passwords in our system — because we never create them. No credential database means no credential breach. Zero attack surface.

Liveness Verified Every Time

Our AI detects depth, micro-movements, and eye signals in real time. Photos, videos, 3D masks, and deepfakes are rejected before they get close.

Cryptographically Verified Identity

Every authentication produces a signed cryptographic proof tied to the user's biometric. Immutable, auditable, and legally defensible.

Zero-Knowledge by Design

Raw facial data never leaves the device unencrypted. We verify identity without ever possessing it — privacy and security in perfect balance.
